# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Review the work and its evidence; avoid insults, harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct or repeated unwanted contact. Do not disclose another person's private information. Different levels of technical experience and language fluency are welcome.

Disagree with specific reasoning or behavior and explain what would resolve the concern. Respect scope, consent and repository ownership. Do not pressure others to share credentials, change access or perform unsafe tests.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close a discussion, warn a participant or restrict participation. They should explain the applicable conduct concern when doing so and protect sensitive reports. Apply these standards to project issues, reviews and other project-managed spaces.

For a conduct concern, use a private maintainer contact surface if one is available. Otherwise ask for a private channel without posting sensitive details publicly. No unverified email address or guaranteed response interval is supplied by this hub.
