Let's dive in to the water-quality pilot.

## Slide 1 — Sampling period
The pilot ran from 1 June through 30 June 2026.

## Slide 2 — Locations
Staff collected samples at three river sites: North Bridge, Market Bend, and South Weir.

## Slide 3 — Sample count
The team collected 24 samples at each site, for 72 samples total.

## Slide 4 — Lab flags
The lab flagged 2 samples from North Bridge and 3 from South Weir for a second review. No samples from Market Bend were flagged.

## Slide 5 — Follow-up
The lab scheduled the second review for 8 July. The pilot has not assigned an owner for the field follow-up.

## Slide 6 — Boundary
The pilot records sample results at the three sites. It does not estimate river-wide conditions or establish a health effect.
