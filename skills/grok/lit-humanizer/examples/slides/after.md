# Water-quality pilot

## 1. Sampling period
The pilot ran from 1 June through 30 June 2026.

## 2. Three river sites
Staff collected samples at North Bridge, Market Bend, and South Weir.

## 3. Sample count
Each site contributed 24 samples; the total was 72.

## 4. Lab flags
The lab flagged 2 North Bridge samples and 3 South Weir samples for a second review. None from Market Bend were flagged.

## 5. Follow-up
The second review is scheduled for 8 July. The pilot has no assigned owner for the field follow-up.

## 6. Scope
The pilot records results at these three sites. It does not estimate river-wide conditions or establish a health effect.
