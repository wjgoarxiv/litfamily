Let's dive in to the museum's ticket policy.

**Refunds:** Visitors may request a refund up to 24 hours before the booked entry time. The request must include the order number.

**Late arrival:** Tickets remain valid for 30 minutes after the selected entry time. After that, staff may admit visitors if space is available.

**School groups:** Groups of 10 or more students should arrive 15 minutes before their entry time. One adult must accompany each group of 10 students.

These guidelines are designed to create a seamless experience for everyone. It is important to note that refunds are not available after the 24-hour cutoff, and late entry is not guaranteed. The policy is not about limiting access, but about helping the museum manage capacity.
