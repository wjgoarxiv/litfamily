# Ticket questions

**Can I request a refund?** Yes. Send the order number at least 24 hours before the booked entry time.

**What if I arrive late?** The ticket remains valid for 30 minutes after the selected entry time. After that, staff can admit visitors if space is available.

**When should a school group arrive?** Groups with 10 or more students should arrive 15 minutes early. One adult must accompany each group of 10 students.

Refunds are unavailable inside the 24-hour cutoff, and admission after the ticket's 30-minute window depends on available space.
