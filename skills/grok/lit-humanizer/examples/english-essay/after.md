The town library began staying open until 8 p.m. on Thursdays in April. During the first four Thursdays, 46 people visited after 6 p.m. Several students also used the reading room after school.

Those figures are an early count, not a forecast. The library can compare later Thursdays with the first four weeks before deciding whether to keep the hours. The attendance sheet records visits, so it does not show how many people came for a particular service or whether the schedule worked for residents who did not attend.

The library should continue the program while it records attendance beyond the first four Thursdays. The current count shows 46 visits after 6 p.m. during that period; it does not describe later weeks.
