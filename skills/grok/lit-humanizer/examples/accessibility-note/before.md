Let's dive in to the keyboard navigation update.

The settings page now provides a more inclusive and seamless experience for keyboard users. Users can press Tab to move through controls and Shift+Tab to move backward. The focused control has a 2-pixel outline with a 3:1 contrast ratio against the adjacent background.

The page respects the operating system's reduced-motion setting. When that setting is active, the panel opens without the 180-millisecond slide animation. The save confirmation remains visible for 4 seconds, and users can reach the close button before the confirmation disappears.

We tested the page with keyboard-only navigation in Chrome and Firefox on macOS. The update is a major step forward in our ongoing commitment to accessibility.
