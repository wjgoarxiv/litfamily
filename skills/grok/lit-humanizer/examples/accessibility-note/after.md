# Keyboard navigation

On the settings page, press Tab to move to the next control and Shift+Tab to move backward. The focused control has a 2-pixel outline with a 3:1 contrast ratio against the adjacent background.

The page follows the operating system's reduced-motion setting. With reduced motion enabled, the panel opens without its 180-millisecond slide. A save confirmation stays visible for 4 seconds, and its close button remains available during that time.

We checked keyboard-only use in Chrome and Firefox on macOS.
