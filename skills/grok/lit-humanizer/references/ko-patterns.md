## Evidence labels

Each entry records the source taxonomy’s evidence status in one of three forms:

- validated: the source describes a comparative corpus result, task-matched result, or measured review set. The entry preserves the source’s genre, model, sample-size, and direction caveats.
- weak: the source offers a linguistic or editorial hypothesis without a direct comparison for that exact cue, or warns that the result depends on model or genre.
- hold: the source explicitly says not to use the cue as a rewrite basis yet.

Validated does not mean “remove this phrase.” The source has several findings where people use a form more often than models. Those entries say so and recommend preservation. A validated measurement is evidence about the measured sample, not a universal authorship rule.
