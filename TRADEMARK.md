# Trademark policy

The code and documents in this repository are free to use under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`). The name
**LitFamily**, the product names LitClaude, LitCodex, LitOpenCode, LitHermes, LitGrok, LitMuse and LitGravity,
and the logos and mascot art are not part of that license. Brand artwork keeps the terms in
[`assets/brand/README.md`](assets/brand/README.md).

- You can fork, change and share the code under MIT.
- If you publish a changed version, give it a different name and logo, and do not suggest that the LitFamily
  maintainers made or endorse it.
- You can say in plain text that your project is "based on LitFamily" or "a fork of" one of its products.
- Unchanged copies, such as mirrors and package caches, may keep the name.
- If your project's name contains "Lit" next to a product or host name, add a line near the top of your README
  saying that it is not made by or affiliated with the LitFamily maintainers.
- The official builds are the seven packages in the README's [Official packages](README.md#official-packages) list:
  the `@litfamily` scope on npm, built from the `wjgoarxiv` repositories with npm provenance. Anything else is
  not an official LitFamily build, whatever its name.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/wjgoarxiv/litfamily/issues.
